    Referensi¶

    Format: gaya APA singkat (penulis, tahun, judul, penerbit atau jurnal).

    Buku teks¶

    • Bishop, C. M. (2006). Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning. Springer.
    • Chapelle, O., Schölkopf, B., & Zien, A. (Eds.). (2006). Semi-Supervised Learning. MIT Press.
    • Géron, A. (2022). Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow (3rd ed.). O'Reilly Media.
    • Hastie, T., Tibshirani, R., & Friedman, J. (2009). The Elements of Statistical Learning (2nd ed.). Springer.
    • Jolliffe, I. T. (2002). Principal Component Analysis (2nd ed.). Springer.
    • Sutton, R. S., & Barto, A. G. (2018). Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (2nd ed.). MIT Press.

    Makalah ilmiah¶

    • Agrawal, R., Imieliński, T., & Swami, A. (1993). Mining association rules between sets of items in large databases. Proceedings of the 1993 ACM SIGMOD International Conference on Management of Data, 207–216.
    • Ester, M., Kriegel, H.-P., Sander, J., & Xu, X. (1996). A density-based algorithm for discovering clusters in large spatial databases with noise. Proceedings of the Second International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (KDD-96), 226–231.
    • Fisher, R. A. (1936). The use of multiple measurements in taxonomic problems. Annals of Eugenics, 7(2), 179–188.
    • Jain, A. K. (2010). Data clustering: 50 years beyond K-means. Pattern Recognition Letters, 31(8), 651–666.
    • Lloyd, S. (1982). Least squares quantization in PCM. IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, 28(2), 129–137.
    • Pedregosa, F., et al. (2011). Scikit-learn: Machine learning in Python. Journal of Machine Learning Research, 12, 2825–2830.
    • Silver, D., et al. (2016). Mastering the game of Go with deep neural networks and tree search. Nature, 529(7587), 484–489.
    • van Engelen, J. E., & Hoos, H. H. (2020). A survey on semi-supervised learning. Machine Learning, 109(2), 373–440.
    • Watkins, C. J. C. H., & Dayan, P. (1992). Q-learning. Machine Learning, 8(3–4), 279–292.

    • Scikit-learn. Unsupervised learning. https://scikit-learn.org/stable/unsupervised_learning.html

    • Scikit-learn. Semi-supervised learning. https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/semi_supervised.html
    • Scikit-learn. Decomposing signals in components (matrix factorization problems) (termasuk PCA). https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/decomposition.html
    • Scikit-learn. Nearest Neighbors. https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/neighbors.html
    • Scikit-learn. Toy datasets (Iris dan Wine). https://scikit-learn.org/stable/datasets/toy_dataset.html
    • Scikit-learn. KMeans (API reference). https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.cluster.KMeans.html
